Ummah Professionals

Online Professional Networking Platform

Project description

Background

Client: Ummah Professionals Org

Date: Aug 2022 – Jan 2023

Type: Web Application

Formerly known as InterMSA, Ummah Professionals has been revitalized to support college students in achieving their professional aspirations. We provide valuable resources, workshops, and mentorship opportunities, fostering growth and connection. Join our empowering community to take the first step towards realizing your career goals.

Problem

The website's ownership transitioned to me as part of a rebranding effort led by Ali Haider, with the aim of transforming its visual and functional aspects. As I was unfamiliar with TypeScript, I faced the challenge of managing my time effectively and engaging in extensive discussions with the team. Together, we prioritized key elements to ensure the website embodied a distinct brand identity.

Solution:

While the website already possessed essential features such as a mailing list and deployment, my task was to enhance their usability and ensure universal accessibility through AWS hosting. By reaffirming these features, I aimed to make the website more user-friendly and convenient for visitors across different platforms.
